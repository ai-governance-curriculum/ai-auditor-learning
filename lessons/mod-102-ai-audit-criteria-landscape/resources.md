> **Freshness note.** This module touches regulation, standards, and professional methodology all of which revise frequently. Treat every `needs-research` tag as a required check at reading time; verify against the issuing body's own site rather than secondary summaries. Where a chapter or an exercise asks you to cite a version, cite the version that is current *and* note it in your workpaper — a criterion that silently moves from one standard version to another between planning and reporting is a report-level risk.
