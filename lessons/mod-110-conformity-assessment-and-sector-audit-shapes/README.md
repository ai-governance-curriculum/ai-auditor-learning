# mod-110-conformity-assessment-and-sector-audit-shapes: EU AI Act Conformity Assessment and Sector Audit Shapes

> Scaffolded by `aicg org execute-plan`. Lecture chapters and exercise content are authored on subsequent autonomous cycles.

**Estimated effort:** 16 hours

## Learning objectives

- Execute EU AI Act Article 43 and 44 conformity assessment as a notified-body auditor would — testing technical documentation (Annex IV), quality management system (Article 17), risk management system (Article 9), data governance (Article 10), accuracy / robustness / cybersecurity (Article 15), human oversight (Article 14), transparency (Article 13), and record-keeping (Article 12); producing the conformity-assessment report, the CE-marking decision, and the surveillance-audit plan
- Execute the EU AI Act Article 55 GPAI with systemic risk audit path — testing model-level safety evaluations, cybersecurity measures, systemic-risk mitigation, and serious-incident reporting against the GPAI Code of Practice; defer frontier-agent methodology depth *up* to `agentic-safety-engineer` (level 40)
- Execute the ForHumanity Independent Audit of AI Systems (IAAIS) engagement against the FHCA criteria — AI Ethics, AI Trust, Algorithm Bias & Discrimination, Privacy, Cybersecurity — for a system in scope under NYC LL144, Colorado AI Act, Illinois AIVIA, or an EU AI Act Annex III high-risk category
- Execute the BABL AI Algorithm Bias Auditor engagement against NYC LL144 — independent-auditor independence test, bias-audit calculation (selection rate, impact ratio, four-fifths rule across race × gender × intersectional categories), summary of bias audit publication, AEDT registration evidence
- Execute a US Federal Reserve SR 11-7 model validation review as the independent-validation auditor — conceptual soundness, process verification, ongoing monitoring, outcome analysis, effective challenge; adapted for ML / LLM / GenAI models at a Fed-supervised institution
- Execute an FDA Good Machine Learning Practice + Predetermined Change Control Plan audit path for an AI/ML-enabled SaMD — testing GMLP guiding principles, PCCP adequacy, Q-Sub / De Novo / 510(k) submission evidence, 21 CFR Part 820 quality-system integration
- Execute an NAIC Model Bulletin on the Use of AI Systems by Insurers audit path — testing AI governance program, risk-management program, third-party AI, consumer-protection controls, state-DOI reporting evidence; execute an Colorado SB 24-205 algorithmic-discrimination audit for a deployer of high-risk AI; execute an Illinois AIVIA independent-review audit
- Execute an SSAE 18 / AT-C 105 / ISAE 3000 attestation engagement on an AI control environment (SOC-for-AI) — scoping description-criteria and control-criteria, testing management's description, testing design and operating effectiveness, authoring the SOC 2-shape AI report
- Choose correctly between the audit shapes above by engagement type — ISO 42001 certification vs. EU AI Act conformity assessment vs. ForHumanity IAAIS vs. BABL AI bias audit vs. SR 11-7 MRM vs. FDA GMLP vs. NAIC vs. SOC-for-AI — defending the choice against the client's regulatory classification and audience
