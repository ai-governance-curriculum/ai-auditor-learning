# mod-111-findings-reporting-and-management-response: Findings Authoring, Reporting, and Management Response Cycle

> Scaffolded by `aicg org execute-plan`. Lecture chapters and exercise content are authored on subsequent autonomous cycles.

**Estimated effort:** 14 hours

## Learning objectives

- Author audit findings in the condition / criterion / cause / effect / recommendation structure (IIA PPF Standard 2410, Yellow Book GAGAS 9.17) — with every finding supported by named evidence, cross-referenced workpaper, and a defensible materiality claim
- Classify findings into major non-conformity / minor non-conformity / observation / opportunity-for-improvement per ISO/IEC 17021-1 for AIMS audits; into significant deficiency / material weakness language for SR 11-7 MRM reviews; into deficiency-in-design / deficiency-in-operating-effectiveness for SOC-for-AI; into pass / conditional / fail for ForHumanity IAAIS; into bias-audit summary language for NYC LL144 publication
- Negotiate management response and corrective-action-plan commitments — challenging responses that defer root-cause, re-opening findings when the response is cosmetic, escalating to the audit committee when management refuses — while staying inside professional-skepticism and independence posture
- Author the audit report in the shape the audience consumes — ISAE 3000 attestation report, AT-C 105 attestation, SOC 2 Type II report, ISO 42001 certification report, EU AI Act Article 43 conformity-assessment report, SR 11-7 validation report, NYC LL144 published bias-audit summary, internal IIA-standard report to the audit committee; include the appropriate opinion type (unqualified, qualified, adverse, disclaimer) and professional-skepticism language
- Deliver audit reports to each audience — opening meeting, closing meeting, management response, audit-committee report-out, board-level reporting, regulator-facing submission, external-stakeholder publication (where required)
- Track findings in a findings-and-recommendations register through to closure — follow-up testing, re-audit evidence, closure memo; feed repeat findings into the next-cycle audit plan; coordinate with the Chief Audit Executive on function-level repeat-finding patterns
- Package regulator-facing submissions correctly — EU AI Act Article 73 serious-incident filing shape, OSCAL (Open Security Controls Assessment Language) representation for US federal audiences, SAR / SR letters for Fed MRM, FDA 483 observation response shape where adapted for AI SaMD; defer legal opinion *out of scope* to counsel
