# mod-101-audit-position-and-independence: The AI Auditor Position, Independence, and Professional Standards

**Estimated effort:** 14 hours

This module installs the professional foundation the rest of the AI Auditor track builds on: where the role sits on the three-lines ladder, the rulebooks you operate under (IIA, ISACA, IAASB, AICPA, ISO), the credentials that signal competence to each buyer, and the signed independence-and-ethics posture you commit to at engagement acceptance. Everything in `mod-102` through `mod-112` and the three projects assumes you can defend this foundation in writing.

## Learning objectives

- Diagram this role's exact scope on the AI Governance level ladder — third line of defense (independent assurance) vs. second line (control design / release assurance, `ai-evaluation-engineer` L35) vs. first line (hands-on engineering craft, `ai-risk-engineer` L25); internal auditor reporting to the Chief Audit Executive vs. external assurance practitioner at a Big-4 / notified body / certification body / ForHumanity-credentialed independent.
- Internalise the IIA **Three Lines Model (2020 revision)** and the IIA **Global Internal Audit Standards 2024** (effective 9 January 2025, replacing the IPPF structure in day-to-day citation).
- Internalise the IIA **AI Auditing Framework** and the IIA **Internal Audit's Role in Governance of AI** guidance as the working shape the role executes inside an organisation.
- Internalise the **ISACA Auditing Artificial Intelligence** body of knowledge and the **AAIA (Advanced in AI Audit)** complement to CISA.
- Read the IAASB framework (**ISAE 3000 (Revised)**, **ISA 500 / 530 / 620 / 320** by analogy) and the AICPA **AT-C 105 / AT-C 205** stack as the external-assurance methodology layer for AI attestations.
- Internalise **ISO 19011:2018**, **ISO/IEC 17021-1:2015**, and **ISO/IEC 42006** as the certification-body audit stack for ISO/IEC 42001 AIMS audits.
- Position the credentials market — IIA CIA + IIA AI-audit certificate, ISACA CISA + AAIA, IAPP AIGP, ForHumanity IAAIS / FHCA, BABL AI, ISO/IEC 42001 Lead Auditor / Lead Implementer, CPA / CA — and defend which combinations are load-bearing for the engagement types this role takes on.
- Author an **independence-and-ethics posture statement** for an AI audit engagement — five IFAC threats (self-interest, self-review, advocacy, familiarity, intimidation), safeguards at firm / engagement / individual / AI-specific levels, and the professional-skepticism test.
