# mod-112-audit-leadership-automation-and-regulator-interface: Audit Leadership, Continuous-Audit Automation, and Regulator Interface

**Estimated effort:** 16 hours

This capstone module covers the senior-practitioner responsibilities of an AI audit professional: leading engagements, operating a continuous-audit model, building the audit function's tooling layer, running the regulator interface, coordinating with external auditors, reporting to the audit committee and board, and sustaining the function's practice over time.

## Learning objectives

- Lead an AI audit engagement at senior-practitioner scope — engagement planning, team deployment (lead auditor, technical specialists, independent reviewer, administrative support), progress tracking, budget / schedule / scope management, quality review, closing meeting, delivery; coordinate with the Chief Audit Executive on engagement-level quality assurance and with external QA on IIA Quality Assessment and PCAOB-style inspection readiness.
- Build and run a continuous-audit operating model for AI — automated evidence collection pipelines (eval-run artifacts, trace-store queries, guardrail-metric dumps, drift-monitor outputs, SIEM AI signals, GRC-for-AI exports, model-registry provenance, Sigstore attestation), continuous controls monitoring, exception-based human review, dashboard reporting to the audit committee; integrate with GRC-for-AI platforms (Credo AI, Holistic AI, ModelOp, Monitaur, ServiceNow AI Control Tower, IBM watsonx.governance, Workiva, AuditBoard, Fairly, Enzai, Trustible).
- Build the AI-audit tooling layer — audit sampling scripts, workpaper templates, evidence-manifest generators, hash-chained log verifiers, automated crosswalk-coverage dashboards; in-source where privilege matters and open-source adoption where the organisation's AI auditor community uses it (OSCAL automation, open-source IA tooling).
- Lead the regulator interface for an AI audit — scheduling, scope negotiation, information-request response posture, interview preparation for management, finding communication, remediation plan tracking; audience-tune for EU AI Office, US AISI, UK AISI, FDA, Fed, OCC, FDIC, NAIC + state DOI, FTC, EEOC, DOJ, FDA Device Center, EU national competent authorities (CNIL, ICO, Garante, BfDI), Singapore IMDA / PDPC, Japan METI, Korea PIPC.
- Lead external auditor coordination — reliance on external audit work (ISA 610 using the work of internal auditors; PCAOB AS 2605 for external-auditor reliance on internal audit for financial-reporting-adjacent AI); coordination with notified bodies, certification bodies, IAAIS auditors, SOC auditors.
- Lead audit-committee and board reporting — pre-read, one-page summary, decision log, risk-heatmap read, dashboard, repeat-finding read, maturity-model trend; coordinate with `head-of-ai-governance` (level 60) as a reporting audience (not a scope this role owns).
- Position the role's own learning cycle — subscription to ISACA Journal, IIA Internal Auditor magazine, IAASB publications, the Frontier Model Forum / Partnership on AI / AISIC updates, ISO technical-committee output (ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42, CEN/CENELEC JTC 21, BSI, ANSI), regulator rulemaking docket subscriptions (EU AI Office, US AISI, FDA, FTC) — treating the audit function as a living practice that the market does not stand still on.
