## Methodology

1. Sourced the canonical task domains for an AI Auditor (level 45, AI Governance family) from the public references catalogued in [`.aicg/job-requirements.json → authoritative_references`](.aicg/job-requirements.json).
2. Sampled 28 live postings in the 2026-08-09 → 2026-11-07 window (see `postings` in the JSON catalog) across the employer archetypes documented in `research_status.needs_research_note`. Filtered out pure financial-statement / pure IT-general-controls / pure analyst / pure engineer / pure CAE postings — kept only postings framed as independent-assurance over AI / AI/ML / GenAI / agentic AI at senior-IC seniority or above.
3. Mapped each theme to (a) the role on our level ladder that should own it primarily and (b) the curriculum module that will cover it.
4. Scored each theme by frequency in the sample (fraction of postings that explicitly cite it as scope) with `evidence_post_ids` listing the postings supporting the frequency figure. The 0.30 explicit-frequency bar is used as a *gate for adding new content*, not as a gate for retaining existing content — mandatory audit steps under IIA IPPF / ISO 19011 / ISA 500/530/620/320 / AT-C 105 / ISAE 3000 / ISO 42006 are retained even when postings don't recite every step.
5. Applied the **ownership rule** — assign coverage to the lowest-level role that genuinely requires the skill; higher-level tracks link back rather than duplicate. Deferral pattern (unchanged from bootstrap) — *down* to `ai-governance-analyst` (level 15), `ml-engineer` (level 20), `ai-infra-junior-engineer` (level 10), `ai-risk-engineer` (level 25); *down/peer* to `ai-evaluation-engineer` (level 35); *down/peer* to `agentic-safety-engineer` (level 40); *sideways* to `ai-eval-engineer` and `model-evaluation-engineer` (peer, level 30); *sideways-and-up* to `security-learning` / `ai-infra-security` (level 35); *up* to `senior-ai-governance-architect` (level 50), `head-of-ai-governance` (level 60), `chief-ai-officer` (level 70); *out of scope* to legal counsel, SecOps/DFIR, Chief Audit Executive, CRO, CCO, audit-committee chair.
6. Compared each explicit-frequency score against the existing 12 modules + 3 projects — concluded that every empirically-supported theme is already covered and no new module/exercise/project is justified this cycle.

## Ownership map — quick reference

- **AI Governance Analyst** (level 15, [`ai-governance-analyst-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-governance-analyst-learning)) — operational analyst legwork. **This audit consumes those artifacts and tests their reliability.**
- **AI Risk Engineer** (level 25, [`ai-risk-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-risk-engineer-learning)) — the hands-on engineering *craft* of AI risk. **This audit tests the outputs that role builds.**
- **AI Eval Engineer** (peer, level 30, [`ai-eval-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/ai-eval-engineer-learning)) — application-layer evaluation-engineering plumbing. **The auditor reads from the trace store + eval pipeline this peer builds.**
- **Model Evaluation Engineer** (peer, level 30, [`model-evaluation-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/model-evaluation-engineer-learning)) — statistical / calibration methodology depth. **The auditor uses this methodology to challenge claims.**
- **AI Infra MLOps** (peer, level 25, [`ai-infra-mlops-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-mlops-learning)) — MLOps automation. **The auditor samples from the MLOps pipeline evidence this peer generates.**
- **AI Evaluation Engineer** (peer / lower, level 35, Governance family, [`ai-evaluation-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-evaluation-engineer-learning)) — release-assurance *methodology* inside the second line. **The auditor independently tests the evidence that peer packages but does not re-author release-assurance methodology.**
- **Security / AI Infra Security** (peer / next-up, level 35, [`security-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/security-learning) / [`ai-infra-security-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-security-learning)) — deep ML/AI security at platform scale.
- **Agentic Safety & Red-Team Engineer** (peer / lower, level 40, [`agentic-safety-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/agentic-safety-engineer-learning)) — frontier-agent red-team methodology.
- **AI Auditor** (this track, level 45, Governance family) — the third-line independent audit + external-assurance craft for AI systems and AIMS.
- **Senior AI Governance / Risk Architect** (next-up architecture, level 50, [`senior-ai-governance-architect-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/senior-ai-governance-architect-learning)) — control-library architecture, policy taxonomy, cross-jurisdiction reconciliation. **The auditor audits against these designs.**
- **Head of AI Governance** (level 60, [`head-of-ai-governance-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/head-of-ai-governance-learning)) — program leadership scope. **Audit-report audience, not a scope this role owns.**
- **Chief AI Officer** (level 70, [`chief-ai-officer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/chief-ai-officer-learning)) — executive scope.
- **Out-of-scope roles** — General Counsel / legal opinion; SecOps / DFIR (SOC-side incident handling); Chief Audit Executive (enterprise audit-function leadership); Chief Risk Officer, Chief Compliance Officer, and audit-committee chair (oversight audiences).
