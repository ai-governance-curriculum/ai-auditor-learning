## Methodology

1. Sourced the canonical task domains for an AI Auditor (level 45, AI Governance family) from the public references catalogued in [`.aicg/job-requirements.json → authoritative_references`](.aicg/job-requirements.json).
2. Mapped each task domain to (a) the role on our level ladder that should own it primarily and (b) the curriculum module that will cover it.
3. Applied the **ownership rule** — assign coverage to the lowest-level role that genuinely requires the skill; higher-level tracks link back rather than duplicate. In particular, deferred:
   - *Down* to [`ai-governance-analyst`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-governance-analyst-learning) (level 15) for the analyst legwork this audit consumes — AI-system intake / inventory, jurisdictional regulatory tracking, first-draft impact assessments, framework-crosswalk legwork, reading eval / audit evidence into governance records.
   - *Down* to [`ml-engineer`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/ml-engineer-learning) (level 20) for classical ML / PyTorch / sklearn / MLflow / packaging fundamentals; to [`ai-infra-junior-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-junior-engineer-learning) (level 10) for engineering-craft prerequisites.
   - *Down / prerequisite* to [`ai-risk-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-risk-engineer-learning) (level 25) for the hands-on engineering *craft* of AI risk — the auditor audits the outputs that role builds but does not re-teach the engineering.
   - *Down / peer* to [`ai-evaluation-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-evaluation-engineer-learning) (level 35) for release-assurance *methodology* inside the second line — the auditor independently tests the evidence that peer packages but does not re-author release-assurance methodology.
   - *Down / peer* to [`agentic-safety-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/agentic-safety-engineer-learning) (level 40) for frontier-agent red-team methodology — the auditor audits dangerous-capability evaluations against public RSP / Preparedness / FSF / GPAI Code of Practice claims without re-authoring the methodology.
   - *Sideways* to [`ai-eval-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/ai-eval-engineer-learning) (peer, level 30, AI Engineering family) for application-layer evaluation-engineering plumbing; to [`model-evaluation-engineer`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/model-evaluation-engineer-learning) (peer, level 30, ML Engineering family) for benchmark, statistical, and judge-vs-human calibration methodology depth.
   - *Sideways-and-up* to [`security-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/security-learning) / [`ai-infra-security`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-security-learning) (peer / next-up, level 35) for deep ML/AI security at platform scale.
   - *Up* to [`senior-ai-governance-architect`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/senior-ai-governance-architect-learning) (level 50) for control-library architecture, policy taxonomy, cross-jurisdiction reconciliation; to [`head-of-ai-governance`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/head-of-ai-governance-learning) (level 60) and [`chief-ai-officer`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/chief-ai-officer-learning) (level 70) for program leadership, board-level reporting, and regulator engagement.
   - *Out of scope* to legal counsel (this role does not deliver legal opinion); to SecOps / DFIR (SOC-side incident handling); to the Chief Audit Executive (enterprise audit-function leadership sits with the CAE — this role is a senior auditor-practitioner reporting into or coordinating with the CAE); to the Chief Risk Officer, Chief Compliance Officer, and audit-committee chair (these are oversight audiences this role reports to).
4. Flagged everything that has not yet been validated against in-window postings so the next cycle can demote any requirement whose evidence stays empty.
