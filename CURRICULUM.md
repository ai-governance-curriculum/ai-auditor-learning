## Overview

This track teaches the *third-line independent audit and external-assurance craft* end-to-end for a senior specialist at level 45: operating under the IIA Global Internal Audit Standards 2024, the IAASB ISAE 3000 + ISA 500/530/620/320 stack, AICPA AT-C 105, ISO 19011 + ISO/IEC 17021-1 + ISO/IEC 42006 → translating ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF + GenAI Profile, EU AI Act (Articles 9-15, 16-29, 43-44, 51-55, 61, 72-73), GPAI Code of Practice, CEN/CENELEC JTC 21 harmonised standards, Colorado AI Act, NYC LL144, Illinois AIVIA, NAIC Model Bulletin, SR 11-7 / OCC 2011-12 / SR 23-4, FDA GMLP + PCCP + 21 CFR Part 820, HIPAA, GDPR Article 22, OMB M-24-10 + M-25-21 + M-25-22, and UK / Canada / Singapore / Japan / Korea / China / India / Brazil AI regulation into *auditable criteria* → constructing an AI audit universe and a risk-based annual audit plan → applying ISA 500 / ISA 530 / ISA 620 / AT-C 105 to AI artifacts (model weights, training data, eval artifacts, inference logs, trace stores, guardrail configs, monitoring dashboards, incident records, change-management evidence) with statistical + judgmental sampling → auditing the ISO/IEC 42001 AIMS to ISO/IEC 42006 certification shape (stage-1, stage-2, surveillance, recertification) with integrated-management-system audit against ISO 27001 / 27701 / 9001 / 14971 / 13485 → independently replicating model / system / dataset card claims (fairness, robustness, red-team, privacy, calibration) using the open-source eval toolchain the `ai-risk-engineer` (level 25), `ai-eval-engineer` (level 30), and `model-evaluation-engineer` (level 30) tracks teach → auditing the organisation's risk-management and red-team / adversarial-ML / fairness / privacy / guardrail programs against AIID / OECD.AI / MIT AI Risk Repository / NIST AI 600-1 / OWASP LLM Top 10 / OWASP ML Top 10 / MITRE ATLAS / NIST AI 100-2 / RSP / Preparedness / FSF / GPAI CoP → auditing continuous monitoring, incident response, and post-market surveillance (EU AI Act Article 72/73) → auditing the AI supply chain and third-party AI (SR 23-4, ML-BOM, SPDX AI, SLSA, Sigstore, in-toto, ModelScan, picklescan, GPAI deployer obligations, OMB federal AI acquisition) → executing EU AI Act Article 43-44 conformity assessment, Article 55 systemic-risk GPAI audit path, ForHumanity IAAIS (FHCA) engagements, BABL AI NYC LL144 bias audits, SR 11-7 independent validation adapted for ML / LLM / GenAI, FDA GMLP + PCCP audits for AI/ML SaMD, NAIC insurer audits, Illinois AIVIA audits, Colorado AI Act impact-assessment reviews, and SSAE 18 / AT-C 105 / ISAE 3000 SOC-for-AI attestation → authoring findings (condition / criterion / cause / effect / recommendation), classifying by engagement shape, negotiating management response and CAP, authoring audit reports in canonical shapes, tracking findings to closure, packaging regulator-facing submissions (Article 73, OSCAL, Fed SAR, FDA 483 response) → leading AI audit engagements at senior-practitioner scope with continuous-audit operating models, GRC-for-AI platform integration (Credo AI, Holistic AI, ModelOp, Monitaur, ServiceNow AI Control Tower, IBM watsonx.governance, Workiva, AuditBoard), and regulator / external-auditor / audit-committee / board interface.

It is a **senior independent-assurance specialist track at level 45**, positioned in the **AI Governance family**, between the AI Evaluation Engineer (level 35, release-assurance methodology inside the second line) + Agentic Safety & Red-Team Engineer (level 40, frontier-agent methodology) below and the Senior AI Governance / Risk Architect (level 50, control-library architecture) above. The differentiator against `ai-evaluation-engineer` (level 35) is **the three-lines-of-defense boundary**: this role operates from the third line (internal audit) or from outside the organisation (external attestation / certification), with defensible independence from the control design and operation it audits. The differentiator against `ai-risk-engineer` (level 25) is that this role **audits outputs rather than authors methodology**; the differentiator against `senior-ai-governance-architect` (level 50) is that this role **audits the control-library the architect designs** rather than designing the library itself.

Total planned commitment: **184 hours across 12 modules** + **140 hours across 3 projects** = **~324 hours**.

## Ownership rule

Following the project-wide ownership rule, this curriculum:

- **Owns** the third-line independent audit + external-assurance craft end-to-end for AI systems and AI management systems — audit position + independence posture, AI audit criteria landscape, risk-based audit planning, audit evidence + sampling for AI, auditing ISO/IEC 42001 AIMS, auditing model / system / dataset card claims with independent replication, auditing AI risk management + harm models + red-team programs, auditing continuous monitoring + incident response + post-market surveillance, auditing the AI supply chain + third-party AI, executing conformity assessments + sector audit shapes, authoring findings + reports + management response, and leading engagements with continuous-audit automation + regulator interface.
- **Defers down** to [`ai-governance-analyst-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-governance-analyst-learning) (level 15) for the operational analyst legwork this audit consumes — AI-system intake and inventory, jurisdictional regulatory tracking, first-draft impact-assessment authoring, framework-crosswalk legwork, reading eval / audit evidence into governance records; to [`ml-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/ml-engineer-learning) (level 20) for classical ML / PyTorch / sklearn / MLflow / packaging fundamentals; to [`ai-infra-junior-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-junior-engineer-learning) (level 10) for engineering-craft prerequisites (Linux, Git, Python, packaging, HTTP APIs); to [`ai-risk-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-risk-engineer-learning) (level 25) for the hands-on engineering *craft* of AI risk — the auditor audits the outputs that role builds but does not re-teach the engineering; to [`ai-evaluation-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-evaluation-engineer-learning) (level 35) for release-assurance *methodology* inside the second line — the auditor independently tests the evidence that peer packages but does not re-author release-assurance methodology; to [`agentic-safety-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/agentic-safety-engineer-learning) (level 40) for frontier-agent red-team methodology — the auditor audits dangerous-capability evaluations against public RSP / Preparedness / FSF / GPAI CoP commitments without re-authoring the methodology.
- **Defers sideways** to [`ai-eval-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/ai-eval-engineer-learning) (peer, level 30, AI Engineering family) for application-layer evaluation-engineering plumbing (traces, judges, RAG eval, online eval); to [`model-evaluation-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/model-evaluation-engineer-learning) (peer, level 30, ML Engineering family) for benchmark, statistical, and judge-vs-human calibration methodology depth; to [`ai-infra-mlops-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-mlops-learning) (peer, level 25, Infra family) for MLOps automation and model-registry depth; to peer AI Engineering tracks ([`llm-application-developer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/llm-application-developer-learning), [`rag-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/rag-engineer-learning), [`applied-ai-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/applied-ai-engineer-learning), [`fine-tuning-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/fine-tuning-engineer-learning), [`training-pipeline-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/training-pipeline-engineer-learning)) for the systems being audited.
- **Defers sideways-and-up** to [`security-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/security-learning) / [`ai-infra-security-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-security-learning) (peer / next-up, level 35) for deep ML/AI security at platform scale — this role plugs AI-specific audit into the broader information-security audit this peer supports.
- **Defers up** to [`senior-ai-governance-architect-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/senior-ai-governance-architect-learning) (level 50) for control-library architecture, policy taxonomy, cross-jurisdiction reconciliation, AIMS design, assurance architecture, evidence architecture, third-party program design, post-market surveillance architecture, GRC-for-AI reference architecture, operating model, and sector blueprints — the auditor audits *against* these designs; to [`head-of-ai-governance-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/head-of-ai-governance-learning) (level 60) for program leadership, board-level reporting, and regulator engagement at enterprise scope; to [`chief-ai-officer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/chief-ai-officer-learning) (level 70) for executive scope.
- **Out of scope** to legal counsel (this role does not deliver legal opinion — it packages regulatory obligations for counsel to interpret); to SecOps / DFIR (SOC-side incident handling — this role audits the interface); to the Chief Audit Executive (enterprise audit-function leadership at enterprise scope sits with the CAE — this role is a senior auditor-practitioner reporting into or coordinating with the CAE); to the Chief Risk Officer, Chief Compliance Officer, and audit-committee chair (oversight audiences this role reports to).

See [`JOB_REQUIREMENTS.md`](JOB_REQUIREMENTS.md) for the requirements-to-coverage map and the cited public references the catalog is grounded in.

## Module summaries

### mod-101 — The AI Auditor Position, Independence, and Professional Standards
Diagram the role's scope — internal auditor reporting to the Chief Audit Executive vs. external assurance practitioner at a Big-4 / notified body / certification body / ForHumanity-credentialed independent. Internalise the IIA Three Lines Model (2020), the IIA IPPF / Global Internal Audit Standards 2024, the IIA AI Auditing Framework, the ISACA Auditing AI body-of-knowledge + AAIA, the IAASB framework (ISAE 3000, ISA 500, ISA 530, ISA 620), AICPA AT-C 105 / AT-C 205, ISO 19011, ISO/IEC 17021-1, and ISO/IEC 42006. Position the credentials market (IIA CIA + IIA AI Audit, ISACA CISA + AAIA, IAPP AIGP, ForHumanity IAAIS / FHCA, BABL AI, ISO/IEC 42001 LA/LI). Author an independence and ethics posture — threats, safeguards, professional-skepticism test.

### mod-102 — AI Audit Criteria Landscape
Translate ISO/IEC 42001 clauses 4-10 + Annex A, NIST AI RMF + GenAI Profile, EU AI Act Articles 9-15/16-29/43-44/51-55/61/72-73, GPAI Code of Practice, CEN/CENELEC JTC 21 harmonised standards, Colorado AI Act, NYC LL144, Illinois AIVIA, NAIC, SR 11-7, FDA GMLP + PCCP + 21 CFR Part 820, HIPAA, GDPR Article 22, OMB M-24-10 / M-25-21 / M-25-22, UK / Canada / Singapore / Japan / Korea / China / India / Brazil AI regulation into testable auditable criteria. Build a crosswalk register. Read IAASB + AICPA AI assurance standards pipelines. Defer control-library architecture *up* to `senior-ai-governance-architect` (level 50).

### mod-103 — Risk-Based Audit Planning
Construct an AI audit universe from the enterprise AI inventory (consuming `ai-governance-analyst` level 15 artifacts). Score audit priority on inherent + residual risk (reusing `ai-risk-engineer` level 25 scoring + audit-specific drivers). Apply ISA 320 materiality tuned for ISO 42001 certification vs. EU AI Act conformity vs. SOC-for-AI attestation vs. internal risk-based audit. Scope AI-system and AIMS engagements. Author engagement plans (team deployment, timing, information-request, interview schedule). Coordinate with the CAE and audit committee.

### mod-104 — Audit Evidence, Sampling, and Workpapers for AI
Apply ISA 500 (evidence sufficiency + appropriateness), ISA 530 (sampling), ISA 620 (auditor's specialist), and AT-C 105 to AI artifacts — model weights, training data, eval artifacts, inference logs, trace stores, guardrail configs, monitoring dashboards, incident records. Author workpapers that survive external QA and PCAOB-style inspection in Caseware / TeamMate+ / Workiva / AuditBoard and in Git-tracked repos for AI-native engagements. Build reproducibility bundles for every independent replication. Audit chain-of-custody and audit-log integrity under professional-skepticism.

### mod-105 — Auditing the AI Management System (ISO/IEC 42001)
Audit ISO/IEC 42001 clauses 4-10 and Annex A to ISO/IEC 42006 / 17021-1 / 19011 certification shape — stage-1 documentation, stage-2 implementation, surveillance, recertification. Audit AI policy + objectives + AIMS scope, risk assessment + treatment process (clauses 6.1.2-6.1.4) consistent with ISO/IEC 23894, impact assessment (ISO/IEC 42005), life-cycle operation controls (clause 8), performance evaluation (clause 9) including internal audit and management review, improvement (clause 10). Integrate with adjacent management-system audits (ISO 27001, 27701, 9001, 14971, 13485) for regulated-organisation integrated audits.

### mod-106 — Auditing Model / System / Dataset Card Claims
Read published model / system / dataset cards (Hugging Face format, Mitchell et al. shape, Gebru Datasheets) as an independent assurance practitioner. Independently replicate performance, fairness, robustness, red-team, and privacy claims using garak / PyRIT / Promptfoo / Inspect; ART / TextAttack / Counterfit; Fairlearn / AIF360 / Holistic AI / SHAP / Alibi / LIME; Opacus / TensorFlow Privacy / ML Privacy Meter / Presidio; HarmBench / AIR-Bench / AgentDojo / InjecAgent / WMDP / Meta CyberSecEval / METR / SWE-bench Verified / MLCommons AILuminate. Apply `model-evaluation-engineer` (level 30) calibration methodology to challenge published metrics. Produce a signed independent replication report. Keep the replication depth bounded — attack authoring stays at `ai-risk-engineer` (level 25) and `agentic-safety-engineer` (level 40); novel-metric authoring stays at `model-evaluation-engineer` (level 30).

### mod-107 — Auditing AI Risk Management and Harm Models
Audit the organisation's harm model against AIID / OECD.AI / MIT AI Risk Repository / AIRO / NIST AI 600-1. Audit the risk register for inherent / residual / control-defeated coherence and sign-off routing. Audit the red-team program (OWASP LLM Top 10, OWASP ML Top 10, MITRE ATLAS, NIST AI 100-2 cross-tagging; garak / PyRIT / Promptfoo / Inspect coverage). Audit the adversarial-ML, fairness, privacy, and guardrail programs. Audit frontier-safety programs against public RSP / Preparedness / FSF / GPAI Code of Practice commitments without re-authoring methodology (defer *up* to `agentic-safety-engineer` level 40).

### mod-108 — Auditing Monitoring, Incident Response, and Post-Market Surveillance
Audit continuous monitoring against ISO/IEC 42001 clause 9.1 + EU AI Act Article 72 using WhyLabs / Evidently / Alibi / Fiddler / Arthur / Holistic AI dashboards as evidence. Audit incident response end-to-end (detect → triage → RCA → post-mortem → regression-fixture → CAP → register update → regulator notification) against EU AI Act Article 73, NAIC, FDA MDR adapted for AI SaMD, and SR 11-7 MRM. Independently replay sample incidents. Audit three-line independence in incident response. Defer SOC-side incident handling *out of scope* to SecOps / DFIR.

### mod-109 — Auditing the AI Supply Chain and Third-Party AI
Audit data / model / training-pipeline provenance (datasheets, HF model cards, ML-BOM with CycloneDX, SPDX 3.0 AI profile, SLSA, Sigstore, in-toto). Audit SR 23-4 third-party risk applied to AI vendors + GPAI providers. Audit reliance on SOC 2 Type II, SOC-for-AI, ISO 42001 certification, ForHumanity IAAIS reports. Audit ModelScan + picklescan model-ingestion gates. Audit GPAI deployer obligations (EU AI Act Articles 51-55 against the GPAI Code of Practice). Audit OMB M-24-18 / M-25-22 federal AI acquisition where applicable.

### mod-110 — Conformity Assessment and Sector Audit Shapes
Execute EU AI Act Article 43-44 conformity assessment as a notified-body auditor; Article 55 systemic-risk GPAI audit; ForHumanity IAAIS (FHCA) engagement; BABL AI NYC LL144 bias audit; SR 11-7 independent validation adapted for ML / LLM / GenAI; FDA GMLP + PCCP audit for AI/ML SaMD; NAIC insurer audit; Illinois AIVIA audit; Colorado AI Act impact-assessment review; SSAE 18 / AT-C 105 / ISAE 3000 SOC-for-AI attestation. Choose correctly between engagement shapes by regulatory classification + audience — this is the hinge skill of the role.

### mod-111 — Findings, Reporting, and Management Response
Author findings in condition / criterion / cause / effect / recommendation structure (IIA PPF 2410, GAGAS 9.17). Classify by engagement shape (major/minor NC, significant deficiency / material weakness, deficiency-in-design / operating-effectiveness, pass / conditional / fail, LL144 bias summary language). Negotiate management response + CAP under professional skepticism. Author reports in the audience's canonical shape — ISAE 3000, AT-C 105, SOC 2 Type II, ISO 42001 certification, EU AI Act Article 43 conformity, SR 11-7 validation memo, LL144 published summary, IIA internal-audit-committee report. Track findings to closure. Package regulator-facing submissions — Article 73, OSCAL, Fed SAR, FDA 483 response. Defer legal opinion *out of scope* to counsel.

### mod-112 — Audit Leadership, Automation, and Regulator Interface
Lead AI audit engagements at senior-practitioner scope (planning → team deployment → quality review → delivery) with CAE and external-QA coordination. Build continuous-audit operating models with automated evidence collection, continuous controls monitoring, GRC-for-AI integration (Credo AI, Holistic AI, ModelOp, Monitaur, ServiceNow AI Control Tower, IBM watsonx.governance, Workiva, AuditBoard, Fairly, Enzai, Trustible). Build AI-audit tooling (sampling scripts, workpaper templates, evidence-manifest generators, hash-chained log verifiers, OSCAL automation). Lead the regulator interface (EU AI Office, US + UK AISI, FDA, Fed, OCC, FDIC, NAIC + state DOI, FTC, EEOC, DOJ, EU national CAs — CNIL, ICO, Garante, BfDI; Singapore IMDA / PDPC; Japan METI; Korea PIPC). Coordinate external auditors (ISA 610 / AS 2605 reliance). Deliver audit-committee + board reporting. Maintain the practice via IIA / ISACA / IAASB / AISIC / Frontier Model Forum / Partnership on AI subscriptions.

## Assessment

Each module ships **1 quiz** plus the exercises and a lab listed in [`.aicg/curriculum-plan.json`](.aicg/curriculum-plan.json). Each project ships a portfolio-grade README, a reproducibility bundle (eval configs, red-team suites hosted per the harmful-payload workflow taught in `ai-risk-engineer` mod-104, model / seed / decoding / prompt / retriever / tool / dataset / metric pinned, workpapers in both electronic and git-tracked systems, findings register with cross-references), and an explicit rubric covering independence posture, criteria-translation quality, audit-planning defensibility, evidence sufficiency + appropriateness, sampling justification, AIMS clause coverage, independent-replication rigour, finding classification correctness, management-response negotiation discipline, report-shape fit, continuous-audit architecture coherence, and regulator-interface posture.

## Where to go after this curriculum

- **`senior-ai-governance-architect-learning`** (next-up architecture, level 50, Governance family) — control-library architecture, policy taxonomy, cross-jurisdiction reconciliation; this is the natural next step for AI Auditors who want to move from auditing against architectures to designing them.
- **`head-of-ai-governance-learning`** (level 60) — program leadership scope; the natural next step for AI Auditors who move into governance leadership (common career move for Big-4 AI Assurance Senior Managers / Directors and for internal-audit AI practice leads).
- **`chief-ai-officer-learning`** (level 70) — executive scope.
- **`ai-evaluation-engineer-learning`** (peer, level 35, Governance family) — natural cross-training for the second-line release-assurance peer, often paired with AI Auditor work in rotating assignments.
- **`agentic-safety-engineer-learning`** (peer, level 40) — natural cross-training for AI Auditors who specialise in frontier-safety audits at labs and GPAI providers.
- **`security-learning`** / **`ai-infra-security-learning`** (peer / next-up, level 35) — natural cross-training for AI Auditors who plug AI-specific audit into broader information-security audit (SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI DSS, HITRUST).
